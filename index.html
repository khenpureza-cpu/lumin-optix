📘 How to Use Lumina Optix

Problem Analyzer Mode:
- Enter a short description of your problem (e.g., “Light travels from air to glass n1=1 n2=1.5 θ1=40°”).
- Automatically detects refraction or lens problems.
- Shows Given, formula used, solution steps, and final answer.
- Saves results to history.

Calculator Mode:
- Choose Snell's Law or Lens Equation.
- Input known values, leave one blank → calculator solves it.
- Snell's Law: n₁, n₂, θ₁ → θ₂ with formula & steps.
- Lens Equation: f, do, or di → missing value with formula & steps.
- Results are saved to history.
